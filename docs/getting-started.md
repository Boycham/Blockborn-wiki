## Your island

Every player starts on their own island with a **Born Block** in the middle of it.
Mine it, and it turns into a new block: dirt, stone, wood and, every so often, a **chest**.

!!! tip "Your first chest"
    The sixth thing you break is always a chest. After that, chests turn up at random.

## Your first tools

1. Break the Born Block a few times to collect **Dirt**, **Stone** and **Wood**.
2. Craft a **Crafting Bench**.
3. Make a **Wood Pickaxe**, then upgrade to **Stone** tools.
