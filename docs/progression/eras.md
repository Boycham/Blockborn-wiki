# Eras

Eras are BlockBorn's progression. Every item and block belongs to one era, and you
unlock eras **one at a time** on the skill tree. You start in <span class="pill era">Era 0</span>.

An era decides what the world will **make** for you: what you can craft, forge and smelt,
and what the Born Block can turn into. It does **not** stop you trading for items or buying
them at the bazaar.

!!! note "Your era follows you"
    Your era is saved on your profile, so it comes with you to every island, the World and
    the dungeons.

## Era 0 <span class="pill era">Start</span>

Dirt, wood and stone. Wood and stone tools, weapons and armour, the Crafting Bench,
chests, signs, doors and fences, saplings, and the wild flowers that grow on your island.

## Era 1

**Iron and coal.** Iron ore, coal, glass, the **Furnace** and the **Anvil**, torches,
and the full iron tool, weapon and armour set. Shards also arrive here.

## Era 2

**Copper, tin and bronze.** Templates for the Born Block, the **Fuser** and **Refinery**,
copper and bronze gear, the **Staff** and **Bow**, and your first machines: pipes, wire,
the coal generator, drills, batteries and the auto-farmer.

## Era 3

**Pyresteel.** Refined bronze, pyresteel and the **Advanced Furnace**.

## Era 4

**Farming and potions.** The pyresteel tool and armour set, the **Tiller**, **Cauldron**
and **Sprinkler**, eight magical crops and the potions you brew from them.

## Era 5

**Advanced machinery.** Voltrite, advanced pipes, the advanced refinery, battery and drill,
and the **Solar Panel**.
