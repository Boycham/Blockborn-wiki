## Your Island

Your home. Build anything you like, break the Born Block for materials, and invite
co-owners to build with you.

## The Plaza

The central island of the World. This is where you'll find the **bazaar**, the
**NPCs** and **quests**.

## The Outer Islands

The islands around the plaza are where the **mobs** live. Bring a weapon.
